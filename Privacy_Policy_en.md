MathStepMath Privacy Policy
Effective Date: December 28, 2025
This Privacy Policy is formulated by MathStepMath (hereinafter referred to as "We"). It sets forth how We collect, use, store and protect your personal information (including information of parents and minor users), as well as the rights you are entitled to. Please read this Policy carefully, especially if you are a guardian of a minor user. You may only use the MathStepMath App (hereinafter the "App") if you agree to this Policy; if you do not agree, please stop using the App immediately.
1. Information We Collect
We abide by the minimum necessary principle and only collect information essential to core App functions:
Learning Information: Your child’s grade, answer records, wrong question data, learning duration (used to match exercise types and generate learning reports);
Device Information: Mobile device model, iOS version, App version, network type, device identifiers (solely to ensure normal App operation and troubleshoot technical issues);
Feedback Information: Problem descriptions and screenshots you voluntarily submit (used to resolve your requests and inquiries).
2. Use of Your Information
The information we collect is used exclusively for the following purposes: enabling account login, exercise answering, learning report generation and other core functions; optimizing product experience; handling customer inquiries and feedback; securing user accounts and preventing potential risks. We will not use your information for any other purposes without your prior consent.
3. Storage and Protection of Information
Your personal information is stored on secure servers located within the territory of China. Account information will be retained for 6 months after account cancellation to resolve residual issues, while all learning data will be deleted immediately upon account cancellation. All information beyond the retention period will either be anonymized or permanently deleted.
We adopt security measures including encrypted data transmission, access permission management and regular security audits to safeguard your information. We shall not be liable for any information leakage caused by your own negligence or improper operation.
4. Sharing and Disclosure of Information
We will never sell or rent your personal information to any third parties. We may only share or disclose your information under the following limited circumstances: with your explicit consent; to fulfill statutory legal obligations; to authorized technical service providers within the minimal necessary scope; or for statistical analysis after the information has been fully anonymized.
5. Protection of Minor Users
This App is designed for elementary school students, and We strictly comply with the Provisions on the Protection of Personal Information of Minors Online. Minor users shall use the App under the supervision of their guardians. The collection of any minor’s information shall be deemed to have obtained consent from their legal guardian. Guardians may contact Us to view, amend or delete the minor’s information, or submit an account cancellation request.
6. Your Rights & How to Contact Us
You (or your legal guardian) reserve the right to access, revise or delete your personal information, and to cancel your account. You may withdraw your consent to this Policy at any time, but you must cease all use of the App upon withdrawal.
